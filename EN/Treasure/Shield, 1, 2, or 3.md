### Shield, +1, +2, or +3

*Armor (Shield), Uncommon (+1), Rare (+2), or Very*

Rare (+3) While holding this Shield, you have a bonus to Armor Class determined by the Shield’s rarity, in addition to the Shield’s normal bonus to AC.
